Why 236 px, exactly

CELL 22, GAP 3, PAD 7, LP_MONITOR_COLS 9 (OutToLaunchCard.svelte:94-97) → 9×22 + 8×3 + 2×7 = 236. The legacy card is width: 300px (:271) because of it.

A live picture is the ruling's first named width earner — but this one earns only 236 px, narrower than most faces in the fleet. No FACE_WIDTH_EXEMPTIONS entry is needed, and the grid must not be scaled up to fill space that does not exist.

The SCREEN switch is required, and it is safe here

video-face-screen-source.test.ts runs over STRICT_FACES ∩ video defs and NO_SCREEN_SWITCH holds only videoOut. This module is in scope and deserves no exemption: its reason to exist is the LEDs, and the canvas is explicitly the no-hardware fallback (out-to-launch.ts:20-21).

⚠ SCREEN OFF cannot darken a performer's hardware, structurally: the 30 fps LED pump lives on node-launchpad-monitor-registry, not on any card or body — which is #1728's entire point. Collapsing skips a drawPreview and nothing else.

⚠ It also adds this module's first node.data write. It must be mutateNode + LOCAL_ORIGIN on the first commit — mutate.guard anchors on .params and cannot see it.
