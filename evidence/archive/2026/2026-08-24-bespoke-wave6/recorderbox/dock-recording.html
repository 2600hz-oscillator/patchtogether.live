it is a RECOVER list, not a take list

The brief called :646-658 a "take list". It is the crash-recovery list, populated by scanRecoverable() from OPFS manifests left by a take that never finalized.

At rest, after a clean session, it is empty and renders nothing — which is exactly what makes the idle dock baseline deterministic. recorderbox keeps no history of completed takes; a finished chunk is a file on disk and the module forgets it.

It sits at the TOP of the body because it is the only thing here with unsaved user data behind it.

⚠ the PRECURSOR, in one paragraph

The registry owns the recording once started. The thing that starts it — $effect on node.data.recording → startRecording(), ~120 lines of folder / presentation-policy / audio-tap / encode-profile resolution — lives on the card (:264-272, :277-…).

recorderbox is NOT in HEADLESS_MOUNT_LANE_TYPES, so a face has no card anywhere. Pressing RECORD would flip node.data.recording and nothing would observe it. The encoder-support probe and the crash-recovery scan die with it too (onMount, :503-513).

Fix: extract to lib/ui/modules/recorderbox-transport.ts — ⚠ not lib/video/, which is the attest basis, following the precedent recorderbox-present-policy.ts:14-17 already sets in as many words.

⚠ the gate that could not have told us

dom-source-modules.test.ts derives both sets from three seams: attachExternalSource(, write(node|id,…), install*FrameDrawer(. All three describe a card that hands the engine a source or pushes pixels in.

recorderbox is a SINK. It could never match any of them. So its absence from both sets is a tautology about a regex, not evidence about its lifetime — and if #1574 were reverted tomorrow the gate would stay green.

The gate is honest about it (its own stated scope, :33-35). No fix is proposed — nothing is broken and the no-new-gates ruling stands. Fixing the stale why is what actually prevents the confusion.
