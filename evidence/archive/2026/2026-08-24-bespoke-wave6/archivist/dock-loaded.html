the picture, live, per node, free

The lane picture is free and per-node, and blank for three of four states. laneGlyphFor returns 'picture' on domain === 'video' alone, and VideoTileThumb takes the nodeId — so the tile costs nothing to author. But it shows the item only for an IMAGE; audio and video items and the empty state all paint the idle gradient. A spec that wrote "the lane shows the loaded media" would be wrong three times out of four. spec.md §4.

the multiplayer defect this found

archivist.ts:184 promises "Multiplayer-aware: the loaded item … mirror on the node so peers see and drive the same item". There is no $effect on item. Enumerated: three effects (adopt, class-toggle, isPlaying-sync) and two onMounts. attachMedia runs on mount and inside loadItem only.

So a remote peer's selection writes node.data.item; the local card updates its attributes and the title link, and never sets .src on any element. The preview stays empty and every output stays idle.

⚠ The sibling has the effect (PeerTubeCard.svelte:482-491). Reading the two together is what found it, and the fix is one effect copied across. It is also the seam the body needs, so the face cannot ship without it. spec.md §11 D1.

the tax it pays

archivist is in DOM_SOURCE_LANE_TYPES, so its real card is mounted in <HeadlessSourceHost> at left:-9999px with pointer-events: none — even while its own dock pane is open.

The engine state survives untouched. All nineteen interactive affordances become unreachable. That is the whole tax, and this body is the payment.
