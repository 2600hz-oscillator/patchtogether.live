⚠ the roster CANNOT be a selector cell

ShellSelectorCell.options is (node) => SelectorOption[] — a pure synchronous function of the node. It cannot await, cannot hold a loading state, cannot render an error.

tvLibrarian's roster is two runtime network fetches against a third-party dataset (TvLibrarianCard.svelte:131, :148 — the data layer says "All network I/O lives in the card" and "Fetched at RUNTIME (we do NOT bundle it)"). Persisting it into node.data to make it expressible would sync a volatile third-party payload into every saved rack.

⚠ The env-for-selectors ask is DISPROVEN (wave 5 BINDERS §9) and is not re-proposed. getActiveEngine() reaches the ENGINE; this roster is on raw.githubusercontent.com, which the engine has never heard of.

the tax it pays

The headless-host tax, as the THIRD member of a settled lineage after cameraInput and loopback. It is in DOM_SOURCE_LANE_TYPES (dom-source-modules.ts:94), so <HeadlessSourceHost> keeps the real card mounted off-screen forever.

That host is what keeps attachExternalSource (:390), the CV trigger poll for next/random (:397) and the roster fetch (:398) alive. Without it the two gate jacks would be patched, visibly connected and INERT.

⚠ Half the debt is already paid: the <video> and its hls.js demuxer are node-owned (node-media-registry, node-hls) and onDestroy deliberately tears down nothing (:423-435).

cost, measured

WebGL attest: THREE basis files (tv-librarian.ts, -data.ts, -geo.ts). face and noUserControl are hash-transparent — but the gain shader fix is not, so PR 1 buys a real-GPU window and PR 2 is free.

ART: ZERO — ⚠ and note the trap: this module has two AUDIO OUTPUTS, so "it has audio, therefore ART" is available and wrong. The gate reads the def's domain, not its ports.

VRT: no baseline exists and the module is in ALLOWED_PERMANENT_EXEMPT. Getting real face baselines needs TWO simPins — the picture and the roster.
