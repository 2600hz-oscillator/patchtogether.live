An empty face is legal and worse than the placeholder. module-face-lint.test.ts:2994 puts a face that ranks nothing explicitly out of scope of the "every promoted LANE TILE paints something" gate, naming flipper and videoOut. So order: [] ships green — and a blank tile tells you less than a placeholder, which at least announces that a real card is one click away. "The gate allows it" is not the same as "it is the answer."
The two cells answer the question you actually have. Looking at a matrix node, the thing you want to know is which two modules is it looking at — and today that costs a dock full-view open, because an un-migrated module renders a moduleShellPlaceholder (legacy-fallback.ts:143-146).

⚠ do NOT add docs? in the same breath

It is the obvious companion and it is wrong twice: module-manifest.ts:42-53 globs audio and video only, so the field would be read by nothing; and matrixMix's absence of docs is load-bearing for module-annotate.spec.ts:107, whose whole subject is that an undocumented module has no Annotate entry.

That fixture survives a face, because NodeContextMenu.svelte:341 gates on hasDocs and a face is not docs.

and why that is not reassuring

strict-docs.ts:300-301 says matrixMix "stays undocumented on purpose — it is the e2e 'undocumented module' fixture." That reads as a reversible policy choice by a person. The operative fact is a missing field on a type, and the two look identical from the comment.

The day MetaModuleDef gains docs?, the mechanical protection vanishes and a sentence in a comment is all that stands between a routine boy-scout edit and a red run. Comments do not gate.

the perf item this promotion inherits

MatrixMixCard.svelte:48-53 is candid: "MATRIXMIX is the one legitimately near-global card … it keeps per-transaction invalidation for now (decomposing it further is a known follow-up)."

Dock-only, that runs when you open it. In the lane it runs always, on every rack with a matrix node. The roster must memoise on a node-set signature, not on docVersion() — a cable moving cannot change the roster.
