⚠ FOR NIBBLES, “KEEP RENDERING” IS LOAD-BEARING, NOT POLICY. The 2026-08-18 ruling says OFF must reclaim the space while the module keeps rendering, and tearing the producer down is the #1720/#1721 class. Here it is worse than a stale frame: surface.draw is where the game ticks (nibbles.ts:584-599 — dt accumulates into tickAccumS, which drives advanceGame()). A collapse that stopped the draw would stop the game and silence every output: three gates, the length CV and two pitched square waves. The toggle gates the CARD-SIDE POLL only. That is the single most valuable assertion in this spec, and it is a permanent test leg rather than a comment.
