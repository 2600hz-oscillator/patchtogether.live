Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. This page draws what a skifree faceplate would be, because the picture is the argument. Three independent blockers — promotion deletes the game, there are zero params to rank, and no determinism seam is buildable inside a vendored pre-built bundle. The audit that produced them found a live product defect worth more than the face.

⚠ SKIFREE HAS NO GAME UNDER THE SHIPPING SHELL, TODAY, ON MAIN. The card injects the bundle <script>, calls window.SkiFree.create({canvas,…}) against its own canvas, and publishes the controller; the factory only READS it. skifree is not in NON_SHELL_LANE_TYPES, not in STRICT_FACES and not in CARD_PRODUCER_LANE_TYPES, so laneRenderKind returns 'placeholder' and no card mounts. No bundle, no controller, no game: the snapshot freezes at its construction defaults, out stays black, and gate — the module's ONLY trigger output — never fires. Expanding the dock pane starts a game; collapsing it calls controller.dispose() and throws the run away, and the dock LRU-evicts a pane when a third module is expanded. Every skifree e2e drives ?shell=legacy, so nothing in CI can see any of it.

the three blockers

#blocker
B1Promotion DELETES THE GAME. STOP-2 rows 3–7 — the bundle <script>, SkiFree.create, the controller publication, the CANVAS itself and MOUSE steering — all live only on the card, and no shell cell can hold any of them. A ShellFileCell loads a file; a ShellActionCell fires an audition; neither instantiates a third-party game runtime.
B2params: [] ⇒ zero controls at every tier. See the plate above.
B3No determinism seam is buildable. The game is a committed pre-built third-party IIFE driving its own rAF with its own clock and RNG. SkifreeController exposes setCursor/enableMouse/disableMouse/reset/dispose/ getState/_forceCrash/_forceEaten — no freeze, no seed, no tick. Adding one means forking the vendored source and re-running the bundle recipe. Its EXEMPT_FROM_VRT entry names the missing hook, and it stays missing.
