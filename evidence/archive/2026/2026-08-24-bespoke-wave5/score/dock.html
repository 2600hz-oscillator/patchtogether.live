⚠ glyph: 'none' is the only literal that compiles

primaryAudioOutPortId (shell-glyph-live.ts:111-113) matches o.type === 'audio'. score's four outputs are pitch · gate · cv · gate — no audio output at all (contract-lock.txt:2807-2810).

So every other literal resolves to a dead {kind:'static'} glyph, which module-face-lint.test.ts:248-308 reddens unconditionally. An author who never thinks about it ships the right thing.

the selection model — the design call

The card is MODAL: fifteen toolbar buttons arm fifteen modes and the staff interprets a click. Fifteen mutually-exclusive arming controls cannot be face cells — whatever they showed, four of five would be lying about one single-valued state.

node.data.selectedNoteId
node.data.noteValue

So the face SELECTS instead. Every mark cell above acts on the selection, which is why TIE is a toggle (turning it off removes the tie — something the card cannot do at all) and DYN has a — option.
