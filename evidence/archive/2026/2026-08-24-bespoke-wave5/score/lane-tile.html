the refusal, mechanism by mechanism

candidateverdictthe mechanism
'picture' — the live video thumb IMPOSSIBLE laneGlyphFor returns 'picture' only for hasVideoSurface(def), which is def?.domain === 'video' (module-shell-model.ts:177-179, :237-240). score is domain: 'audio'.
'scope' | 'meter' | 'envelope' | 'waveform' DEAD GLYPH primaryAudioOutPortId (shell-glyph-live.ts:111-113) matches o.type === 'audio'. score's four outputs are pitch · gate · cv · gate — it has no audio output at all. Every literal falls to {kind:'static'}, which module-face-lint.test.ts:248-308 reddens unconditionally.
'algorithm' — a bespoke extension glyph WOULD BE IDENTICAL PER NODE ShellExtensionGlyphProps (shell-extensions.ts:39-52) carries num, numbers, testid and no nodeId, so every score in the rack would draw the same picture — and on this module the score IS the instrument.
the picture a player would actually want MORE THAN nodeId BUYS the useful glance is "where is the playhead in the piece?" — which needs data.notes + data.pages plus a per-frame engine read (read('currentNoteId'), score.ts:706-723). That is node data and a live handle, not a discrete param value.
⚠ THIS IS THE SAME MECHANISM kria REFUSED ON, and that is the finding rather than a weakness. kria's own face comment (kria.ts:199-207) reads: "a nodeId prop alone would still not be enough here — the picture a player wants is the playhead over the SELECTED track's SELECTED lane, which is two more pieces of node.data." score is that sentence with "page" substituted for "lane".

Two independent instruments landing on one structural refusal is stronger evidence than two different refusals would be — and these are the two modules whose lane picture would be the most valuable in the whole roster. The glyph seam's limit is a SHAPE, not an oversight waiting for a prop.

how the lane roster is derived

laneOrder(face) =
  face.order
    minus face.hero.cell
    minus each xyPads[].x

score declares a hero.cell and no pads, so the staff panel is dropped and everything else survives to every tier. PF-22 is what lets the picture rank FIRST at no lane cost — kria is named in its own comment as the first adopter, and score is the second.

Tier caps are GEOMETRY, not an authored ladder (curated-face.ts:75-80, resolving through module-shell-model.ts:366-368 and :473): mini 1 · compact LANE_ROW_MAX_CELLS = 3 without a glyph / 2 with · full PLATE_COLS × PLATE_MAX_ROWS = 6 · dock Infinity. score declares glyph: 'none', so compact gets three. ⚠ The tier is named 'full', not 'plate' — it is the LANE's biggest tier, not the dock.

the lane never paints the staff

A panel cell is DOCK-ONLY by a NAMED rule: panelCellKeys() (shell-cells.ts:2045-2058) filters on kind === 'panel' because "a 280 px SVG has no business being SELECTED into a 46 px lane knob column".

score's staff is 720 px. The rule was written for a picture a quarter that size.

⚠ what the LANE does not fix

midi-learn-note.spec.ts boots /rack?shell=legacy&seed=none, and shellFaces = false routes laneRenderKind to 'legacy' — the verbatim card, promoted or not.

So after promotion that spec keeps finding score-play-ds-1, keeps binding NOTE 60, and keeps passing — on a surface no default user reaches. It does not go red. It goes green and blind, and it would certify the orphaned-binding bug as fine.

The fix is CLAUDE.md's: fix the SUBJECT. Keep the legacy leg, re-titled to say what it now proves, and ADD the leg on the default shell that can actually fail.
