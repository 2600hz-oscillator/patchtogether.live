FROGGER — dock faceplate · the board

Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. One band, one knob and a fullViewBody — the cheapest face in the wave. FROGGER is not a game you play: it is a five-input event transformer whose transfer function is a road and a river, and the verb is aim the hops.

⚠ What ships today, and why this face is the fix. The board is drawn by the CARD. Frogger is not in NON_SHELL_LANE_TYPES, not in STRICT_FACES and correctly not a CARD_PRODUCER — so under the shipping shell laneRenderKind returns 'placeholder', the card is not mounted anywhere, and a rack shows a blank grey tile for a GAME. Compact by construction. A 200 px board and one knob column (40–68.8 px measured across the live dock) — an expected plate of ~230–260 px, among the narrowest in the fleet. Nothing here earns a wide plate and the board must not be inflated to fill one. That is the compact default working, not a defect.

zone map

zonewhat it is
bodythe 14×13 board, painted by drawFrogger — the def's own pure exported function, imported, never re-implemented. A PF-14 panel cell would be the natural seam and cannot be used: a panel's first legal rank is 7 and frogger has ONE param.
the HUD⚠ painted inside the canvas, and ALLOWED. Lives, level, timer, score and the PRESS START banner are the module's ARTWORK — the game paints them, not the face. A chrome row beside the board would be the hero readout strip with a different label and is refused by name. ⚠ The resting-text gate can see NEITHER shape (canvas text is its own named blind spot), so this dock baseline and a human are the whole enforcement.
SCREENoverlaid in the board's own box on a translucent backplate, never a row of its own (a stacked row cost spirographs ~18.8 px against ~11 px of slack). ⚠ Unusually safe here: the game runs on the SCHEDULER, engine-side — and that scheduler is a Web Worker setInterval with no AudioContext check — so OFF stops a drawFrogger call and nothing else. One module away in this same group, skifree does NOT have that property.
bandrun — one band, one control. order and pages agree, which is unusual and is stated so nobody hunts for the disagreement. No tab rail (DOCK_TAB_MIN_BANDS is 7) and face.tabbed is owner-instruction-only.
rearauthored: steer (four direction gates) + start. The five gate inputs have no paramTarget — they are the module's real signal inputs, not CV holes — so without a group the rail would be five anonymous jacks.

controls & primitives

keyprimitive & why
initialTimerank 1 · 'knob', the generic default, DECLARED NOTHING. On a module whose outputs are gates this is the one control that changes the OUTPUT RATE: with nothing steering, DEAD fires every initialTime seconds like a very slow LFO. ⚠ NOT declared 'fader' — FroggerCard renders a <Knob> while its sibling modtris renders faders, and each face matches its own card.
—⚠ No units and no landmarks. A format on the param makes the readout PAINT, which would re-introduce a resting decimal under the dial by the back door — mechanism five. The seconds live in aria-valuetext.
freezeNEW · noUserControl, writer:'internal'. Required by the VRT seam and nothing else. Without it the Push card goes from one encoder to two, the second labelled “stop the game”.
—⚠ No START button, and it is the obvious missing affordance: a player whose frog runs out of lives has no way to restart from any surface without patching a cable. Refused because it does not exist on the card either — adding it is a FEATURE, not a promotion. Its future shape is an ACTION cell with an AUDITION-LEDGER probe, never a revision counter.

The determinism seam CLOSES A NAMED RATCHET, and it is cheaper here than anywhere. frogger's EXEMPT_FROM_VRT entry states its own exit condition: “Promote to a real VRT baseline once a deterministic-time test hook is added so the scene can freeze the game at a known tick.” This PR builds it — and grep "Math.random|rng|seed" frogger-state.ts returns zero hits, so the board is already a pure function of TICK COUNT and the pin needs no seed at all.
