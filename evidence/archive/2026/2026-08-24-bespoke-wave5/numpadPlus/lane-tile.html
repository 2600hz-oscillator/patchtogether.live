⚠ THE MINI TILE IS ONE KNOB, AND THAT IS WORTH THE OWNER SEEING BEFORE MERGE. On a module called "numpad+", a 192 px tile showing a single four-position dial is thin. The obvious remedy — a lane picture of the sixteen steps — is refused for platform reasons, not design ones (right column), so this is what the geometry gives. M7 in the spec routes it to owner preview rather than letting it be discovered after promotion.
Why activeLayer and not isPlaying at rank 1. numpadPlus sounds while stopped — tick() returns early on !isPlaying but still calls applyOutputs, and the def's own docs say "When stopped the playhead holds at step 1 but live keys still sound". So the transport is not the gate on making a sound, and the layer is: it decides which pitch/gate pair the keys drive, which layer records, which layer the grid edits and which layer feeds POLY. It is also the module's only CV-addressable control, and the disambiguator when a rack holds the several instances the def explicitly supports ("chord-stack style").

glyph: 'none' is the only literal that compiles into a green run. An author who never thinks about it ships the right thing.

and the picture it WOULD want needs more than a nodeId

The useful glance is "what is on the active layer, and where is the playhead". That is not a picture of a SIGNAL — it is a picture of node.data, needing the node's layers, its activeLayer and a per-frame engine read of stepIndex.

ShellExtensionGlyphProps carries num, numbers and testid — no nodeId — so waves 2 and 3's escalation would not be enough here either. kria's face reaches the identical refusal by the identical arithmetic (kria.ts:199-208). Two independent sequencer faces agreeing is the strongest form of that argument, and still not a reason to build the seam on a module PR.

⚠ check the four-position dial, do not reason about it

At every lane tier paramCellKind returns 'knob' for an optioned param, so activeLayer is a four-detent dial and octave a nine-detent one.

moog962 shipped a two-detent dial and faces-parity failed it on both attempts — "dragging the knob commits a param change into the graph". Four should clear it. M2 drives the control instead of trusting that sentence.
