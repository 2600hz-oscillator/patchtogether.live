§1 — WHAT THE MODULE IS FOR

The verb is ALIGN. Up to six SURFACES, each a draggable quad (corners TL,TR,BR,BL in normalized [0,1] output space); surface i is fed by in(i+1); composited painter’s-order OVER into one output → a projector. GRIDS-FIRST: a fresh mappy shows ONE surface, and any live surface with no input renders its NUMBERED calibration grid — set geometry on the physical faces first, then patch video and the grid swaps to the warped feed in the quad you mapped. Per-surface FIT (zoom-fit) vs CROP (native window) toggle; per-surface reset; export/import of the venue layout as JSON. Camera-assisted auto-align is a later phase BY DESIGN — no camera input, no CV.
