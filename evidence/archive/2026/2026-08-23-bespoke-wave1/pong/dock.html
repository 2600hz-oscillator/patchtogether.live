PONG — dock faceplate · STATE 1: the court

Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. One band and a fullViewBody. Pong is not a game with CV bolted on — it is a two-input, two-output signal processor whose transfer function happens to be a game, and the gates fire on a derived event rather than a divided clock.

state 1 · the dock court state 2 · the lane tiers
⚠ What ships today, and why this face is the fix. The court is drawn by the CARD. Pong is not in NON_SHELL_LANE_TYPES, not in STRICT_FACES and correctly not a CARD_PRODUCER — so under the shipping shell laneRenderKind returns 'placeholder', the card is not mounted anywhere, and a player looking at a rack sees a blank grey tile for a GAME. The game itself keeps running (it is subscribed to the scheduler, engine-side) and the gates keep firing. Every pong e2e drives ?shell=legacy, so nothing in the suite has ever looked at this.

zone map

zonewhat it is
bodythe court, painted by drawPong — the def's own pure function, imported, never re-implemented. A PF-14 panel cell would be the natural seam and cannot be used: a panel's first legal rank is 7 and pong has three params.
the score⚠ painted inside the canvas, and it is permitted. It is not derived state about a control; it is the picture itself, the same kind of object as the ball. Text drawn into a canvas is the resting-text gate's own named blind spot, so this distinction is argued here rather than assumed.
SCREENoverlaid in the court's own box. ⚠ Unusually safe here: the game runs on the SCHEDULER, engine-side — not in the card, not on rAF — so OFF stops a drawPong call and nothing else. The gates keep firing. The next audio module with a body will NOT have that property, so it is a permanent test leg rather than a comment.
bandmatch — one band. All three params are the same idea (the rules of the match, set before you patch anything) and splitting them would invent a distinction the module does not have. order and pages agree, which is unusual and is stated so nobody hunts for the disagreement.

controls & primitives

keyprimitive & why
speedrank 1 · paramCells:'fader', DECLARED. On a module whose outputs are gates, the tempo is rank 1 by definition — everything downstream is clocked by how long a rally takes. ⚠ Ranked 1 despite the latency defect: ranking descends from what the module IS, and ranking around a bug you are fixing in the same PR bakes the bug into the UI permanently.
paddleHrank 2 · fader. The strongest runner-up in the wave, and it nearly won on a measurement: it is the only param read EVERY STEP rather than only at resetState, so it is the only one whose effect is visible on the next frame and applies to the rally in progress. It loses because it changes DIFFICULTY, not RATE, and a rack cares about rate.
serveAnglerank 3 · fader. Genuinely third — it does nothing to a rally in flight and its effect is statistical. Its 0 endpoint is a dead-horizontal serve, which is a feature for a repeatable rhythm and a trap for anyone who wanted variety.
freezeNEW · noUserControl, writer:'internal'. Required by the VRT seam and nothing else. Without the declaration it would be a fourth turnable param and the Push card would offer “stop the game” under an encoder.
—⚠ No SERVE button, and it is the obvious missing affordance. Refused because it does not exist on the card either, so adding it is a FEATURE rather than a promotion — and an ACTION cell needs a probe a feature-less module cannot supply.

The determinism seam CLOSES A NAMED RATCHET. Pong is in EXEMPT_FROM_VRT, and that entry states its own promotion condition: “until either (a) a deterministic-time test harness is added so VRT can freeze the ball at a known position, or (b) the prototype is promoted out of research/.” This PR builds (a): a freeze early-return plus a __pongVrtSeed simPin. The game is already a pure function of tick count — dtSeconds is a constant, never a measurement — and both initPongState and stepPongState already accept an injectable rng. So the card's exemption should be re-examined in the same PR, and if it is dropped, ALLOWED_PERMANENT_EXEMPT loses pong in the SAME commit.
