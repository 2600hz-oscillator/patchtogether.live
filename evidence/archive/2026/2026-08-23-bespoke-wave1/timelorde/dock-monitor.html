TIMELORDE — dock faceplate · STATE 2: STOPPED + MUTED, live video monitor

Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. Two changes from state 1: a cable is in video_in so the display is a monitor and the owl steps aside, and the transport is in the worst of its four states — halted and muted.

state 1 · running · owl state 2 · stopped+muted · video monitor
⚠ This is the state the module exists for, and it is why the two rosters are adjacent. Measured on the real clock core, 4 s at 120 bpm: running=0, muteOutputs=1 and both together are byte-identical on all thirteen gate outputs — zero edges, zero peak, zero DC. Nothing downstream of a patch cable can tell them apart. The only surface that separates all four states is these two option rosters, side by side. Delete either roster and the plate can no longer answer “why is my whole rack silent”.

⚠ The negative control, spelled out. Moving bpm from 10 to 300 must leave both of those strings byte-identical. A readout that were secretly paramId:'running' would also not move on bpm — so the sharper leg is the other one: with running=0 fixed, flipping muteOutputs 0→1 must move control-running's string from the STOPPED text to the STOPPED+MUTED text. A single-param readback cannot do that.
SCREEN OFF is a producer question here. The card's rAF is the sole writer of displayFrame. If OFF stopped it, drawFrame would fall back to the #07090d idle field and video_out would go dark for everything downstream — a preview switch acting as a producer kill switch. So OFF unmounts the visible canvas and the compositing loop keeps running. The card is already structured that way; the correct implementation is the one that is already there.
