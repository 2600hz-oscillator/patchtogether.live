why a fader, not a dial

The card mounts a NeonFader (AudioOutCard.svelte:226). Undeclared, it silently becomes a dial — 'fader' and 'hue' are the two primitives that cannot be inferred.

A master level is a throw, not a dial. mixmstrs and noise are the precedent, and noise's face carries the declaration for exactly this reason.

the selector is not a new mechanism

ShellSelectorCell's first line is "A dropdown over a NAMED roster that lives in node.data (not a param)" — and its options is a function of the node, evaluated at render.

milkdrop is the precedent for a genuinely live list: "live list rather than a static roster, because the list grows with in-session .milk imports". A device list that changes on devicechange is the same shape.

two disabled states, and the card cannot tell them apart

The <select> disables on devices.length === 0 || !setSinkIdSupported (:195) but explains only the second (:214).

So a supporting browser with zero enumerated devices shows a greyed (no outputs) and no reason at all. The face's disabled aria-valuetext names which cause, and the model test asserts both.

no SCREEN ON/OFF here

dockscope, spectrograph and samsloop each refuse one on videoOut's argument: when the picture IS the module, collapsing it deletes the product.

A face with this meter collapsed is a face with one fader on it, and the reason to open AUDIO OUT is to see the level. Do not add a switch whose off state is an empty plate.

no lane picture, and the refusal is mechanical

audioOut declares outputs: []. primaryAudioOutPortId is null, so every glyph literal falls to dead static and the unconditional dead-glyph clause catches it.
