SCREEN OFF IS NOT A FREEZE. The state lives on node.data.previewCollapsed (never component $state — this body unmounts on dock collapse and LRU eviction), so it survives a tab switch, a remount and a reload, and syncs to collaborators. what the CV range switch actually does

statelaw
AUDIO (0)px = sample × halfHeight — a ±1 sample fills the half-height
CV (1)px = (sample / 5) × halfHeight — a ±5 V pitch sweep fills the same height
