⚠ THE ZOOM/ROT PAD IS THE WEAKER OF THE TWO, AND THE SPEC SAYS SO. Its axes have different curves and ranges — zoom is log 0.3..3, rot is linear ±1 — so equal pixel travel is not equal parameter travel in the two directions. The mechanism permits it (each axis maps its own param), and the card already ships it. The alternative — split it into two faders — loses no gesture, because nothing couples the two axes. That is an owner question, not an agent decision: flattening a 2-D pad is exactly the loss face.xyPads exists to prevent, and doing it deliberately for an incommensurate pair is a different case from doing it by accident. spec.md §7.1 / §11.

⚠ THREE CONTROL FAMILIES, NOT ONE. The card’s strip is a swatch, two <select>s and a file button — but a family key resolves ONE ShellCell. So wavesculpt-osc splits into wavesculpt-preset-{n} / -table-{n} / -load-{n} (two selectors and a file), and the swatch leaves the family entirely to become a paramCells: 'color' cell. Splitting controlFamilies is FREE — the field is stripped from the WebGL attest hash. A single panel was refused because a panel requires a probe and a picker’s only observable is the selection it makes.

⚠ the defect this tab makes visible

On the LOOK tab, the card’s VIEW button paints 3D / BIRDSEYE / SPECTRO from a hand-written ternary (WavesculptCard.svelte:3148), while VIDEO_MODE_OPTIONS declares PROXIMITY / BIRDSEYE / SPECTROGRAPH — and VIEW_CYCLE_TITLE, the title on that same button, is built from the roster. One element, two vocabularies; two of three states disagree. The def’s own comment says the rosters exist so “one roster [is] read by the def, the dock and the card”, and every other consumer complies. A segmented cell fixes the faced surface for free; the legacy caption needs the one-line repair. spec.md §9.1.
