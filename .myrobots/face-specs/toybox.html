§1 — WHAT THE MODULE IS FOR

The verb is COMPOSE. Four layers, each its own FBO: shader/gen (MIT GLSL bank, lazily fetched, per-content float params), obj (bundled CC0 OBJ or procedural primitive, matcap-shaded, depth-tested), video (inA/inB cable, local file, or camera), or off. A combine DAG blends them (fade/lumakey/chromakey/map) into one output. Six modsignal inputs are ROUTED — each addressed to a layer/combine/obj param with a bipolar attenuverter + offset, with always-on inline scopes showing the live values. A named preset store (save/select/export/import) captures the whole console. Persistence: node.data.layers + node.data.combine + node.data.cvRoutes (+ image bytes) ride the Y.Doc; the factory reads the live node each frame.

§5.1 Width — earned twice over

The composite preview + the console (layer strip, combine graph, CV scopes) is the fleet’s deepest body; the plate is max-content of the console at its own internal max-width with internal overflow:auto where sections outgrow it (“a clamp CLIPS a wide face where a scroll REVEALS it” — the scroll box is INSIDE the component, never a plate-level clamp). Expect zero FACE_WIDTH_EXEMPTIONS entries.

§6.5 The media-controller tax — what toybox actually pays

the one precondition — #1589, closed for the shell world

The discriminator (“is the thing the body needs to show and drive IN THE GRAPH?”) splits toybox’s media three ways: image bytes YES (Y.Doc — no tax); video-file elements and camera tracks NO (browser-local resources named by graph state). Today the CARD owns them with retry timers — so the LRU-evict/collapse unmount kills live layers, the exact #1589 P0, mitigated but not closed. The face PR ports ownership to a node-keyed registry (node-extras-registry / node-video-source-registry family — the repo already ships the pattern and its behavioural proof, extras-producer-lifetime.spec.ts): elements and tracks live for the NODE, swept with the graph, the body adopts-and-reads. Export reads the registry’s truth, closing the “Exported … zero video bytes” lie a second way. This is the entire content of needs-media-controller for toybox — a tax already invented, twice, by cameraInput (headless host) and audioIn (#1590 registry); toybox takes the registry form because its media is per-LAYER, not per-card.
