Why this is BLOCKED and not "two dead controls"

Both params are read by the worklet, and alpha's job is to blend the fourth (HOLO) wavetable into the field. If the HOLO table never arrives, alpha is correctly a no-op — and the tables ride port.postMessage, whose delivery is not guaranteed inside an OfflineAudioContext rendering faster than real time. The macrooscillator route (esbuild the worklet against stub globals, hand the processor its tables synchronously) settles it in half a day, and it must happen before a faceplate paints ALPHA as a working control.

Independently measurable now: a −0.375 DC offset at every setting

DC −0.375433acRms 0.348243−0.751 at level 2

The offset is larger than the signal. Present at every setting of every control; only wrap = 1 changes its sign. Same class as the cube-rebuild blocker, except cube's fault lives at two knob extremes and this one is the default state. ⚠ Not determined: whether a DC blocker sits downstream on the master bus.

And the module is not reproducible run-to-run

max|run1 − run2| = 8.453e-1 on identical params, against 0.000e+0 for every other module in this batch. Usually a deliberate random start phase — but it means hypercube can never hold a byte-exact ART baseline, and the first pass's "alpha does something, Δ = 8.45e-1" was reading phase noise.

What twelve modules found about the platform

Four asks, in priority order

1 · A faceplate needs a way to say "this control is asleep, and here is what wakes it"

Sixteen mode-gated controls across four modules — swolevco's two, wavecel's five, warrensspectrum's one, cofefve's seven. Every one is documented in docs.controls; none is visible on a panel. Three of the four specs spend their whole sidebar on this. A first-class enabledBy on ParamDef, or a FaceReadout that can read connectivity, would replace four bespoke sidebars with one platform behaviour.

2 · Five of twelve modules have NO audio output, and the glyph taps the first one

timelorde, illogic, featurecv and ninelives have none at all. Batch 4 found this twice and asked for a declared glyph source; this batch found it four more times in twelve modules, which makes it the most common face-blocking gap in the unfaced tail. Two specs work around it (a param-derived 'waveform' glyph; 'none') and both workarounds are strictly worse.

3 · The rank-7 wall, quantified

A panel's first legal rank is 7. Four of twelve cannot reach it — destroy (3 keys), illogic (4), ninelives (2), unityscalemathematik (5) — and three of those four are modules whose entire face value is a picture. The sidebar custom block covers it, and it is now the majority pattern for small modules rather than the exception.

4 · Two modules independently wanted the same panel

ninelives and timelorde both need a divider ladder — N rungs, each labelled with its period, at a fixed ratio. Different props (divisor 3, taps 9 vs divisor 2, taps 13), same drawing. Registered once as rate-ladder it serves both, plus every moog9xx divider. First time two modules in one batch converged on one panel without being asked to.

Validate the instrument

The measurement was wrong four times, and every wrong answer looked authoritative

Each had something moving — a passing negative control — and was still measuring the wrong thing.

ModuleWhat the bad instrument saidWhy it was wrong
wavecel"the whole ADSR and BASE are dead — Δ = 0.00e+0 on three outputs"The factory reads livePatch.edges, not bus presence. And with the corrected rig, a gate high from sample 0 landed in the same quantum as the k-rate trigger_connected write — so ATTACK and DECAY read dead twice. Delaying the gate to t = 1 s produced a textbook 1 s ramp. SUSTAIN and RELEASE moved throughout.
hypercube"alpha does something — Δ = 8.45e-1"The module is not reproducible run-to-run. max|Δ| was reading phase noise. The tell was incoherence: the same Δ at 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1, with acRms identical to six decimals.
timelorde"SWING does nothing — 7 pulses at 45° and 7 at 90°"A pulse count is invariant to swing by construction — swing moves when an edge lands, never how many. Intervals: 562.5/437.5 vs 625.0/375.0 ms.
illogic · unityscale"the attenuverter at −1 and +1 are the same — dead control"RMS, peak and centroid are all invariant to a sign flip. Identical to every printed figure; Δ = 1.80e+0 and 1.60e+0 — exactly twice the amplitude.

The meta-lesson: prefer a positive control against a known magnitude — a four-source brightness ladder, a 1 s attack that must take exactly 1 s, a 5 : 3 swing ratio — over a negative control that merely proves the needle can twitch.
