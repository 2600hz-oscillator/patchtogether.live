THE ARMED BANNER IS GONE AND NOTHING WAS LOST. The card painted listening… press a control to bind A (Esc to cancel) as a sentence with a live dot beside it. The dot was already the non-text half — wave 5 §2.1's activity-dot principle — and the ARMED CELL itself already carries class:armed, so which control is listening is on screen without a word. The sentence becomes the armed cell's aria-label, and role="status" moves onto that cell. Escape still cancels, and its window listener plus its teardown ride the body.

⚠ The sweep box REPLACES four live numbers — and improves the affordance

The card printed x [−0.98, 0.97] · y [−1.00, 0.86], four live measurements at rest and the sharpest violation on the module.

Half the finding never needed the number. complete calibration is disabled={!canComplete}, driven by sweepIsUsable — so “am I there yet” is already answered by a control's enabled state, on a non-text channel, today.

The other half — how close — is now a picture. One bordered <div> positioned from sweep.minX/maxX/minY/maxY through the same dotX/dotY mapping the live dot uses, inside the pad that already draws a crosshair the same way. It grows as you sweep; edges mean done. The numbers go to aria-valuetext, so nothing is unassertable.

⚠ It must stay a <div>. A WebGL canvas would enrol this file in the GPU attest automatically; a 2-D canvas would make the body picture and hide every measurement it draws from the resting-text gate. Neither belongs here.

The MAPPING lamp — the “it's transient” argument, refused

mappingStatus self-clears after 4 s, so there is a real argument that a toast is outside a rule about text at rest. Refused anyway: a rule whose strength is that it has never granted an exception is weakened most by the most reasonable one, and “transient” is unbounded — every readout can be re-timed into it.

And refusing costs nothing here, which is the honest reason. The lamp carries the whole message in detail, distinguishes SUCCESS from REJECTED in tone (colour, the primitive's stated design), and is more persistent than the toast — a user who looked away for five seconds currently misses ignored: invalid JSON entirely.

The remap FSM, unchanged

Right-click an LED or a trigger label → arm only:'button'; press Remap X/Y under a stick → arm only:'axis'. The armed listener DIFFs consecutive polled snapshots through detectChangedControl and binds the single control that moved most past a 0.5 threshold. Esc or an 8 s timeout cancels.

The shipped NXT Gladiator preset needs ten remaps — including buttons at indices 19, 21, 23 and 25 — plus invert: { ry: true } and both stick calibrations. That is what this surface exists to produce.

⚠ The write that is NOT clean

Seven of eight node.data writers on this card go through mutateNode. The eighth is setPadIndex (GamepadCard.svelte:393-397): a bare proxy write to t.params.padIndex, with no ydoc.transact and no LOCAL_ORIGIN. Cmd-Z cannot undo a slot change.

⚠ The face fixes it as a side effect, because the ranked SLOT cell writes through the standard param seam — so fix it explicitly and name it in the PR, or the defect vanishes into a refactor and the ledger entry with it.
