what survives, and why each is allowed

Lane numbers 1–8 and GRID / CLIP / ARR / CTRL — option NAMES inside the controls that select them, with aria-pressed already correct on the card (:218, :239). Connect Push 2 / Connect display / Bind — control captions on their own buttons. The two error messages — absent whenever nothing is wrong. The pre-Connect hint — instructional copy in an empty state, replaced by the surface the moment the surface exists.

⚠ And the whole 960×160 canvas. Wave 5 GAMES.md:59-65: "The face is not painting the number; the game is." Here it is stronger than anywhere else in the fleet — deleting those readouts would not remove a readout from the product, because the hardware still paints it. It would only make the on-screen copy disagree with the panel, which is the single property the shared-seam design exists to make impossible.
