Width — nothing is earned, and nothing is asked for

No live picture, no scope trace, no video preview, no XY pad — the four things the ruling names as genuine earners. face.hero is not declared, and face-width-source.test.ts gets no entry (its PLATE_FLOOR_EXEMPTIONS is empty and stays empty).

es9 is a wide-BAND face, not a wide PLATE: fourteen cells on one row is content, not reserve. The gate measures bodyW − contentW ≤ 40 px against modes at 15 and 32-33 px, and cells wrap before they clip — hiddenX === 0 is the leg that catches a clip, and it is in the required lane.

Three bands. No tab rail.

DOCK_TAB_MIN_BANDS is 7 and face.tabbed is owner-instruction-only. Three honest bands render as a column, and that is correct — the type's own doc-comment says so. Nothing is padded to reach a rail and nothing is merged to avoid one.

The glyph is meter, and es9 is the cohort's only member that gets one

primaryAudioOutPortId matches type === 'audio' exactly (shell-glyph-live.ts:111-113). Every other binder — midiCvBuddy, midiOutBuddy, chromaconsole, midiclock, midiLane — has no audio output, so all five are forced to 'none'. es9 declares sixteen, so a live meter on in1 binds.

⚠ That makes es9 evidence against the sixth-glyph platform ask being cohort-wide. The honest population for "binding state + event rate" is binders whose payload is EVENTS, not binders — because the one that moves AUDIO already has a picture.
