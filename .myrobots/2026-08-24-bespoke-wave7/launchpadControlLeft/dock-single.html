⚠ What genuinely narrows, said plainly

Today the lane shows this status line. After promotion the lane shows two buttons and the status lives in a dock-only lamp's aria-label. "This rack's Launchpad is driving THAT clip-player" goes from zero clicks to one.

That is not a parity violation — a status readout is not an affordance, and the ruling refuses it. It is a reduction in at-rest information, and CLAUDE.md requires naming which finding lost its surface. On a rack with two clip-players it is the only thing distinguishing them. Recorded as a decision, not an accident.
