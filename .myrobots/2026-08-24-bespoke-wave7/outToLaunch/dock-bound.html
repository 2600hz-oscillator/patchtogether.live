⚠ What the two knobs actually do — and where a grep would have lied

FRAG_SRC declares exactly two uniforms, uTex and uHasInput. There is no uBright and no uGamma, and surface.draw never reads the params. Grepping the shader says DEAD — the wave-6 gain class.

They are alive on the CPU, applied to the 81 bytes after the readback: node-launchpad-monitor-registry.svelte.ts:262-266 reads them off the ENGINE (ve.readParam, never a card's props) and hands them to monitorGridToLeds; the preview calls the same rgb8ToLp(v8, bright, gamma). So a face cell writing either one reaches the hardware with no card mounted — the two knobs work on a promoted module by construction.

⚠ The face body must import rgb8ToLp rather than re-derive it. The registry already states the rule for these exact two numbers: the helper defaults gamma to 1 while the def's default is 2.2, so falling through would render a different picture than the LEDs.

⚠ The P0 spec this state belongs to

launchpad-monitor-survives-card-collapse.spec.ts drives this exact flow on the DEFAULT shell — Connect, pick, MONITOR ACTIVE, then collapse and prove the surface stays lit, claimed and pumping. Promotion reddens its "real card mounted in the dock full-view" leg and silently re-points its "the shell renders a placeholder tile" leg at a different world.

The face PR owns rewriting it against the face, keeping every node-side and device-side assertion, its 120 s bounded-failure cap and its frame-counted polls exactly as they are. spec.md §8.
