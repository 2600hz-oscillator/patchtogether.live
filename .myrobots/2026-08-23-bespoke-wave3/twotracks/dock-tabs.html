Losing it is a real parity loss.

seven bands — and the decision's sensitivity is stated

Two reels × (TRANSPORT / TAPE / TONE), plus MIX. The owner's ruling is that "lots of controls of DIFFERENT types" gets a tabbed face; this module has knobs, faders, toggles, segmented selectors, actions and two canvases.

⚠ A reviewer could reasonably collapse TAPE + TONE per reel, which drops the count to three and turns the rail off. If that happens, do not re-split to get the rail back — that is the padding the rule forbids. Ship untabbed (ruttetra's precedent) or raise the threshold question to the owner.

the two drag seams, and the split is already right

One canvas, two gestures, and the card gets the seam correct:

START / END markers
  setNodeParam(id, 'start_a', clampLoopStart(...))   :207
  → durable, undoable, synced

PLAYHEAD scrub
  port.postMessage({ type: 'seek', reel, pos })      :217
  → transient engine state, NOT in the Y.Doc

The face must preserve that exactly. Making the scrub a param would put the playhead in the Y.Doc at frame rate — the CV write-storm class.

the transport probe is free

sendTransport posts to the worklet port and writes nothing to the graph — the ShellActionCell shape. But the engine already mirrors the result back: transportState_a / _b land in node.data (twotracks.ts:444-454).

So the cells take a data probe, which the registry says to prefer — and which is stronger than an audition ledger because it observes the engine's result rather than the message's departure.

SAVE TAPE — and the precedent carries a warning aimed at this module

samsloop's export cell (shell-cells.ts:787-798):

twotracks' SAVE TAPE sits in the same reel block as REC, on the same node — the exact adjacency that comment describes. Get it wrong and the probe is green because the user pressed a different button.

the LEDs — a judgement, with a revert

The eight lamps are derived state. The ruling's four deletions were all of labelled values, and an LED is a live picture, not text — so they survive as lamps.

But the meaning must not be painted as a word, and armed vs rec vs overdub go to aria-valuetext on the transport cell, where they are distinguishable in a way four similar lamps arguably are not.
