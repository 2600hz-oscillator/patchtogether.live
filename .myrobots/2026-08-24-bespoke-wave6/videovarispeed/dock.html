⚠ the picture is BLITTED — the <video> is never adopted

The card adopts SEVEN node-owned elements through nodeMedia.adopt (:1037-1072), and its rAF transport drives slotEls[activeSlot]. A DOM node has one parent — a body that adopted slot 0 would take it OUT of the card and videoEl would go null mid-transport.

So the body calls blitOutputForPreview(nodeId), exactly as LoopbackOutputBody.svelte:178-219 does. Verbatim constraint, seven times the blast radius.

the tab-rail question, refuted by the census

This card is 1710 lines and looks like the wave's control-heavy candidate. The ranked census is THREE, giving TWO bands, against DOCK_TAB_MIN_BANDS = 7.

Everything else on that card is node.data-backed DOM living in the body, and a body is not bands — dockTabPlan counts post-hero bands and a shell extension contributes none. Padding pages to reach seven is the named anti-pattern.
