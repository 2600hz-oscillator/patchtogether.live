why the result list is not a selector

ShellSelectorCell.options is (node) => SelectorOption<string>[] — a pure function of the NODE. The roster is card-local $state, not on node.data and not on the engine handle.

⚠ This is not wave 4's env question. That was refuted in BINDERS.md §1 — getActiveEngine() already reaches the engine from plain .ts. No capability reaches a Svelte component's $state, so the fix is to move the state, not to widen a type.

And after the registry moves it, three objections still stand: a SelectorOption is {value,label} with no thumbnail; the cell needs a controlFamilies entry, which IS in the contract; and a ranked selector competes with gain for band space when it is a library, not a control.
