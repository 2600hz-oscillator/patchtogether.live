Face batch 3 — twelve proposed dock faceplates

SPEC AND MOCKUP ONLY. Nothing here is implemented and no module def is touched. Drawn against the PF-20 faceplate platform on feat/faceplate-platform-v2 (PR #1301, not yet merged): face.title / face.hint, a per-band hint, face.hero (picture + promoted control + audition + readouts), a declarative face.sidebar, and FaceReadout.valueId for derived numbers.

Every value under a knob is a readout. A readout tinted green with a ƒ is DERIVED — computed from the DSP's own law over the live params, not a readback of the knob it sits under. Each one's negative control (the perturbation that moves it while a knob readback would not) is stated in the strip below the drawing and argued in full in the per-module spec.

Full specs: .myrobots/plans/face-specs-batch-3-<module>.md · index and CI arithmetic: face-specs-batch-3-INDEX.md

Three of the twelve I am recommending you DO NOT build. noise does not deserve a face on merit — one param, zero inputs, zero modes, so all four face tiers would be identical. cube and twotracks are my two additions to reach twelve, and both come back as swap-outs: cube because seven of its twenty-four knobs do not survive measurement (MORPH is bit-exactly dead at spawn), twotracks because twelve of its thirty-one params are inert until a take exists and its transport is not params at all. All three are drawn below anyway, clearly marked, so the call is yours and not mine.

analogVco

analog-vco.ts · 6 params · 9 in / 6 out new face

One phase accumulator, six taps. The face exists to say which of the six outputs each knob addresses — shape touches only the MORPH tap and pw's authority over it is max(0, 2·shape−1), which at the shipped default is zero. Today all six sit in one undifferentiated fader row with nothing saying so.

bluebox

bluebox.ts · 12 params · 12 gate in / 1 out new face

Twelve momentary keys into one bank of ten sine oscillators. Two keys that share a tone column stack into the same slot — which is why {1,4} is 1.76 dB louder than {1,5} although both hold two keys and light four tones. The hero bank is the only surface on which that becomes visible. ⚠ Its face.order is declaration order and says so in the def: a telephone keypad has no principled priority ranking, so the lane's information is the glyph, not a subset of the keys.

derived · level
√( ½ · Σ_f (0.25·n_f)² ) — verified against the shipping worklet to 4 dp on five cases. Negative control, and it is the best in the batch: {1,4} vs {1,5}. Both hold two keys, both light four slots, both peak at ≈1.0. Every count-based readback returns the identical number; the true RMS differs by 1.76 dB because 1 and 4 share column 1209.
derived · headroom
−20·log10(0.25·T), T = total tones held. One digit = −6 dBFS. Two digits = exactly 0 dBFS. Three clips. Negative control: add key 5 to key 2 — they share column 1336, so the peak goes 0.5 → 0.75, not → 1.0. A per-key model predicts 1.0 and is falsified.
the honest weakness

already wrong

clap

clap.ts · 10 params · 5 in / 1 out new face

The strongest candidate in the batch. A clap is two envelopes on one noise source: a burst train of N onsets, and a room tail that starts at the last onset, not at the strike. macrooscillator

macrooscillator.ts · 6 → 9 params · 8 in / 2 out partial rewrite

Fourteen engines behind one four-knob scheme. The rewrite adds three params (strike_decay, strike_colour, aux_level) and a patched-sensing strike gate: unpatched, the module behaves exactly as today; patch the trigger and each edge fires a pitch-tracking attack into a gate that blends filter↔VCA. It also moves the fourteen engine names into the def as ParamDef.options, killing two duplicate tables. aux_level defaults to 1.0 — that is not taste, it is the compatibility contract: AUX is unscaled today, so any other default silently rewrites the level of an output already patched into people's racks.

saved racks

samsloop

samsloop.ts · 4 → 7 params · 4 in / 1 out new faceblocked

A one-shot / looping sample player with a bolted-on recorder — no overdub, no feedback, no quantisation. Two things must land first. (1) The three recorder settings live in node.data, which no face block can express, so they need promoting to real ParamDefs. REC persists, redraws, round-trips and downloads, and the module stays silent. A face whose third band is called "recorder" over a recorder that cannot be heard is worse than no face.

derived · three tap levels
white = L/√3, pink = L·√17/(√3·17), brown = L·√(1/12)/(8√(1−0.99²)). Negative control: change NORM from 1/8 to 1/4. A knob readback prints "0.50", unchanged; the derived brown moves −17.87 → −11.85 dBFS and its crest headroom flips from 5.3 dB to −1.3 dB (clipping). rejected readout, and why
A spectral-slope number. Nothing about the slope is controllable — there is no morph knob — so "−3 dB/oct" would be a constant string. Decoration, not a readout. Naming the rejection because it is the obvious idea and it is wrong.
already wrong
Four wrong numbers in comments and tests ("RMS ~3.5" is 2.05; "peaks under ±1" measures 1.1234; "steady-state RMS ~0.4" is 0.257), a doc that describes brown as a flat −6 dB/oct when it is white below 77 Hz, and an undisclosed 0.5 Hz loop-seam tick on brown in 169 of 200 seeds.
a vacuous test
noise.test.ts asserts pink noise "can briefly exceed ±1". Sixteen rows plus one white, divided by 17, is hard-bounded by 1 by construction — measured peak over 400 buffers is 0.711 and zero samples exceed 1.0. The assertion cannot fail for the stated reason.

cube

cube.ts · 27 params · 15 in / 4 out my additionrecommend: swap out

A static 3D scalar field ray-marched by one movable plane — the 256 samples that plane reads are the waveform. Genuinely beautiful, and its card visualiser is the only one in the batch that is provably correct (its cut plane is built from the same rotation the DSP uses). But seven of twenty-four knobs do not survive measurement, and they share one architectural cause: the ray integrates over a window centred on its own origin, so translating the plane along its normal is very nearly a no-op. Fix the DSP first; it would be an excellent face afterwards.

derived · stereo width
20·log10( rms((L−R)/2) / rms((L+R)/2) ). Negative control, and it is the killer: SPREAD at 1.00 reads "100 %" while the measured width is −36.2 dB and corr(L,R) = 0.999979. The knob is a lie and only a derived readout says so.
derived · turning points
Sign changes of the posted cycle, already on the main thread. Negative control: MATERIAL SMOOTH → HARD moves turning points 28 → 50 while rmsΔ is only 0.021 — the count sees a timbral change RMS misses.
rejected readout, and why
A "beat frequency between two rotation planes". The three rotations are static Euler angles evaluated once per slice render — there is no angular velocity and no time term. It is the obvious readout for a module called cube and it would be an invention.
and a cost no other module has
cube declares rendersWebGL: true and is in the WebGL attest basis, so the face block must be wrapped in docs-hash-ignore markers or the edit churns the GPU attest hash. It is also the only module in the batch with zero linux VRT protection today.

twotracks.ts · 31 params · 10 in / 2 out my additionrecommend: swap out

A two-reel varispeed tape deck. Real material for a face — eight derivable numbers, three with clean negative controls — but twelve of its thirty-one params are inert until a take exists, the face-critical interactions (REC / PLAY / STOP / SAVE / seek / scrub) are not params at all, and reel B's ECHOES knob is a permanent no-op because echoes_b was never declared as an AudioParam. derived · overdub ceiling
p∞ = a/(1−d) where d = 0.1^(1/echoes): +0.9 dB at 1 echo, +5.4 dB at the default 3, +8.7 dB at 5. Negative control: hold every knob still, engage OVERDUB and let it loop — the tape peak climbs pass by pass while an ECHOES readback prints a motionless "3".
derived · bus gain
20·log10(gainA + gainB) → 0 dB at ab 0, +6.02 dB at 0.5. Negative control, textbook: sweep ab 0 → 0.5 and gainA is invariant at 1.0 the entire way — the card's existing "A:100 %" readout literally cannot move across half the control's travel while the bus climbs 6 dB.
a readout that is impossible — say so
A truthful pitch figure. The record-time rate is baked into the medium and the module stores no recordRate, so record at 2× and play at 1× and the knob reads 1× while the tape sounds an octave down. Printing a number here would be a guess wearing a formula.
already wrong
Reel B's ECHOES does nothing, ever — echoes_b is not a declared AudioParam, so it falls back to 3 and the host write optional-chains into a no-op. A 186-line dead module contradicts the shipped worklet twice and two sweep exemptions cite its tests as their justification. 48 kHz is hardcoded twice, so on a 44.1 kHz context the readout is 8.8 % wrong and exported WAVs play 8.8 % fast.
