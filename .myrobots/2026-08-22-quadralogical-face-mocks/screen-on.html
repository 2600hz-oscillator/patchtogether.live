The previews cost no new GLSL. QUADRALOGICAL already renders a second FBO every frame — a 2×2 tile of the four RAW inputs, in1 TL / in2 TR / in3 BL / in4 BR, with a thin separator cross — and already exposes it as the preview output port (PREVIEW_FRAG_SRC, quadralogical.ts:575-611). The face is one call to blitOutputPortForPreview(nodeId, 'preview'). Its quadrant map is already the quadWeights corner map, so the previews land under the corner labels by construction — subject to the flip check in the red panel below.
⚠ MUST-VERIFY before this ships: the blit's vertical origin. PREVIEW_FRAG_SRC works in vUv with a bottom-left origin (bool top = vUv.y >= 0.5) while a canvas is top-left. If blitTexToDrawingBuffer does not flip, IN1 lands bottom-left and silently contradicts the corner label directly above it — a wrong picture under a right label, which is worse than no picture. Prove it with a per-quadrant colour probe, never by eye.

the puck recovers the MIX (spec §4)
Requirement 4 removes the standalone preview, which showed the MIX. The quadrants show the four RAW INPUTS. Those are not the same picture, so swapping one for the other would leave a MIXER's faceplate unable to show its own mix. The puck already exists, already has a position, and already costs the layout nothing — so it becomes the window. Two blits per frame do not fight: blitOutputPortForPreview keys cadence on (node, port) (engine.ts:1689) exactly so a surface can show a primary preview and a second port at full rate. VIDEOCUBE's SLICE is the precedent. Revert: drop the puck canvas — and the MIX then has no surface on the faceplate at all.
what SCREEN OFF means here, and why it differs
The 2026-08-18 ruling says OFF collapses the preview and reclaims its space while the module keeps rendering. On all twenty-six adopters the collapsed thing is a canvas. Here the frame is also the CONTROL, so collapsing it would delete the joystick from the dock outright — and a declared pad never reaches a lane tier either (laneOrder makes every pad anchor dock-only, because a lane knob column is 46 px and a pad is square), so the module's primary gesture would have no surface anywhere. OFF therefore unmounts the canvas, keeps the overlay, and reclaims 120 px of WIDTH rather than the box.
⚠ two things the build must prove
(1) The blit's vertical origin — see the amber panel above. (2) Pixel determinism: a live 2×2 video preview is not deterministic. freeze is the module's own hook and freezeFaceVideo is the harness, but the dock scene must be shown identical across three consecutive captures before any baseline is trusted. That is the analogVco lesson: its glyph drew a genuinely moving saw and measured 254 / 154 / 315 px across three captures of the same tile.
why there is no tab rail
Three bands; the rail engages at DOCK_TAB_MIN_BANDS = 7. That is not a shortfall — it is required. A rail shows one band at a time, so a tabbed face would put at most one edge box on screen and directly contradict the owner's "the four EDGE boxes move below the screen". face.tabbed is owner-instruction-only and is not reached for.
