Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. The face is three bands and a fullViewBody. The body is the module's own 220×220 2D display — the beat-pulsing owl, or a live monitor of video_in — plus the fleet-standard SCREEN switch overlaid in its bottom-right corner, never in a row of its own.

controls & primitives

keyprimitive & why
runningrank 2 · segmented from a new options roster. The roster is what lets the plate print RUNNING — an option NAME is the one kind of resting text still permitted.
muteOutputsrank 3 · same. GATES LIVE / MUTED.
timelorde-taprank 7, dock-only · ShellActionCell, mode:'trigger'. Its probe must press TWICE — TapTempo locks on the second tap, so a one-press probe cannot tell a warming-up controller from a dead button.
bpmrank 1 · knob, log 10..300. Not a fader (no throw), not a warped fader (the card does not convert — it passes the def's own numbers).
swingAmountrank 4 · knob. Drawn dim: at its 0 default the swing output is an exact copy of its source.
swingSourcerank 5 · selector, 12 options > the segmented cap of 6. Today it has no roster at all and a faceplate would print a bare integer — the defect this promotion fixes.
wizardOnrank 6, painted by the body. Last on purpose: it switches a picture that only exists on the dock.
