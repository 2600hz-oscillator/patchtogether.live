Spec and mockup only; nothing here is implemented. Two bands and a fullViewBody. One knob for the generator, three for the camera — and three params that are on the plate today and must not be.

state 1 · screen on state 2 · screen off
⚠ The promotion is a FIX-PLUS-FACE, and the fix is the bigger half. cv_grow, cv_reset and freeze are synthetic jack/harness params with no card control and no noUserControl declaration. That is not a face blocker — it is a live defect on two surfaces the face has nothing to do with: the Push 2 generic card gives all seven params an encoder, and the group instrument bar auto-exposes all three as knobs. Turning cv_grow once latches gated mode permanently and the RATE knob is dead forever with no badge; turning freeze stops the module rendering. The declaration is three lines and it is hash-transparent, so it costs no GPU re-attest.

the rank, argued

#why this rank
1 ratethe only control over the generator. Turn it to 10 and the frame fills in seconds; at 0.5 the same garden takes twenty times as long to become the same picture. ⚠ DEAD whenever GROW is patched — and that gate is a permanent latch.
2 viewthe camera control with a performance verb. Near plants shift 5× further than far ones, so a sweep is a dolly, not a crop.
3 horizonthe most violent control and still rank 3, deliberately: at 0 the depth sort collapses and every plant anchors at y = 0. A dramatic extreme is not the same as a control anyone rides.
4 fovlast for a structural reason, not an aesthetic one: it is the only visible param with NO CV input. A param nothing can automate is set-once by construction. Its shipped 0.7 also reproduces FAR_SCALE = 0.22 exactly, so a fresh spawn shows nothing about it.

SCREENaria-pressed="true", and a title saying the module keeps rendering — and, on this module, keeps GROWING.
⚠ The style monitor was designed and REFUSED. This module has FOUR live outputs — mono, watercolor, psychedelic, clean — rendering the same plant set every frame, and every surface shows only clean. A four-way port picker would be the obvious win. It does not work: clean always renders, and the other three render only when something downstream is connected. A monitor pointed at watercolor with nothing patched shows a never-written FBO — black on first boot, a stale frame after. A picker that silently shows a lie is the [GATED] badge defect rebuilt on purpose. The right fix is a video-engine change (teach the module that a previewed port counts as connected) and it costs a real GPU re-attest, so it is its own PR.
Attest: ZERO — and conditional. lushgarden.ts and lushgarden-scene.ts are both in the 218-file WebGL basis. But face, noUserControl, docs and controlFamilies are stripped before hashing, and this PR touches only those. Adding units or a landmark to any param would move the hash — so the spec refuses all three candidates and records horizon = 0 as the one to take on a free ride.
