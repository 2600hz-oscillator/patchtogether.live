The width is EARNED, and only here. CLAUDE.md names four genuine earners — a live picture, a scope trace, a video preview, an XY pad, and a control that appears in one mode only. A picker over a grouped roster of 27 needs ~200 px; a knob needs ~68. The mode is what lets the resting plate be narrow and the editing view be wide, so the plate never carries width it is not using.

Slot 7 is STALE, and the sentence it replaces was worse

The card prints “N slot(s) point at controls this device no longer has. Reassign them.” (:221-223) — a derived sentence containing a derived count. The face marks the cell, in both modes. status.staleSlots already names WHICH, so this is strictly more information than the card gives, not less: the card tells you how many, the face tells you which one to fix.

⚠ The card’s comment is right about why the signal must exist at all — “a slot that quietly behaved like an empty one would leave automation writing into a dead lane with nothing anywhere saying so.” Keeping the signal while dropping the sentence is the whole move.

The groups are authored data — keep them

The descriptor has 35 controls in five groups: primary (8), secondary (8), modules (4, role: 'enum'), bypass (6, mostly enum), other. The card builds GROUPED at :95-103 and renders one <optgroup> per group at :245-251.

A flat 27-item list is unusable. The grouping is not decoration; it is how a player finds “the movement one” among four things called movement.

Why the label shortens on the knob but not in the picker

knobLabel() (:157-169) takes the tail after · — amount · character becomes character on the dial. Its doc says why: “the qualifier is already visible in the select directly above it, so the tail alone is unambiguous — and the full string overlaps its neighbours at this width.”

⚠ That argument DOES NOT SURVIVE the mode split. On the card the select is directly above the knob; in a mode, the two are never on screen together. So the perform view must show the qualified name, or a player reading character cannot tell amount · character from effect vol · character from the modules control literally named character — three different controls in this roster.

This is the kind of reasoning that transfers silently and wrongly: the shortening is correct given a layout the face does not have. Perform-view captions stay qualified; the 4-across grid gets two lines instead of one.

PUSH ALL and the actions need a PROBE

shell-cells.ts:157 makes ShellActionCell.probe required. An audition writes nothing to the graph, so readParam/readData are structurally blind — faces-parity once asserted toBeEnabled(), clicked, and asserted nothing, and sixstrum shipped a face over an instrument that could not be sounded.

chromaconsole already has the right instrument. read('ledger') (card header :28), and chromaconsole.spec.ts:240 already proves “with NO output selected, a write sends nothing and is RECORDED undelivered” — delivered: false recorded, never dropped, which is exactly the shape the probe contract wants.

Carry the testids across unchanged

All node-scoped, all already correct. Do not invent new ones — a renamed testid turns three mechanical e2e re-points into a behavioural review. Six of the module’s ten tests need no change at all, because the suite was written against behaviour rather than markup.
