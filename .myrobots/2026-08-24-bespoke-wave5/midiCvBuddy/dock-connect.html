Why one hint dies and the other lives

stringverdictthe reason
“Click to grant MIDI access (one-time per origin).” DELETE It explains what a labelled button does — the button already says Connect MIDI…. It is painted at rest, always, which is the shape every deleted mechanism had. And it already exists verbatim in docs.explanation, reachable by right-click Annotate.
“Permission denied…” KEEP The ruling governs the RESTING faceplate. This appears only after a failed action, on a plate that otherwise never shows it — it is the outcome of a gesture, not resting state. Deleting it leaves a button that does nothing with no way to find out why, on the one path where the browser has actively refused.

The line that separates them

Every mechanism the owner has deleted was permanently painted: the decimal under every dial; the sidebar on every face; the hero strip on 50 of 68 faces; a caption a heading already conveyed. None was conditional on a failure. That is the distinction this split rests on, and it is the ruling’s own word — resting.

⚠ And no gate can check it. face-resting-text-source.test.ts denies ModuleFace FIELDS with no permitted text role — it reads the TYPE. Text inside a fullViewBody is invisible to it, exactly as text drawn into a canvas is. This call is enforced by the dock VRT baseline and a human reading it, and by nothing else. Saying so is required; implying coverage is not available.
