2 · what is FORBIDDEN — the list a builder must be able to recite

Every one of these is a thing somebody would plausibly add to a score face, and every one is refused.
  • a playhead position readout — bar 3 · beat 2, 12 / 64, 0:14
  • a note count — 47 notes
  • the tempo as a decimal under the BPM knob — the mechanism deleted 2026-08-17
  • a transport word — PLAYING / STOPPED beside the toggle
  • a dynamic percentage — the card's title renders 55 %; a title is unpainted and may stay, a painted one may not
  • a standalone page counter — the card's .page-counter span
  • a "score is empty" hint on the plate — instructional copy belongs in the empty PICTURE
  • any hover-reveal of the above — "i want the data gone, not there but hidden", refused by name

4 · what may NOT be added to the picture, even though it looks like notation

The blind spot cuts both ways: a derived readout wearing notation's clothes is invisible to the gate too. Bar numbers, a tick ruler, beat subdivision marks, a "page 2 of 4" watermark, or a tempo marking derived from bpm would all be derived chrome. A real engraver draws bar numbers; this module does not, and must not start.

Say so in the PR body, so the person reviewing face-score-dock.png knows what they are looking for.

⚠ three things the card advertises and does not have

(a) deleteNote has exactly one call site — ScoreCard.svelte:540, a keystroke on the FOCUSED note. Tab is consumed by the rack flip (workflow-pins.ts:306-312, bare Tab), and pointerdown preventDefaults. No e2e test anywhere exercises note deletion.

(b) docs.controls['score-note-{n}'] says "click a note to select/remove it". There is no select and no remove — and score is in STRICT_DOCS.

(c) addTie is the only writer of d.ties. A tie can never be removed except by deleting one of its notes, which per (a) is itself unreachable.

These are one bug, not three: score has no selection model, so every per-note operation was hung off browser focus — and browser focus was taken away by an owner ruling in a different file.

the panel's probe

probe: {
  testid: 'score-staff',
  action: 'click',
  effect: { kind: 'data',
            key: 'notes',
            expect: 'changed' },
}

data, not data-rev — the registry's own warning is that "a revision-only probe passes on a DEAD button that bumps the counter".

And it works in the shipped default state with no setup: a fresh score has notes: [] and noteValue absent ⇒ 'quarter', so ONE click on empty staff writes a note and notes changes.

the grid is IMPORTED, never re-derived

score-data.ts:148-151, in its own words:

"A re-typed copy of 'which ticks does the scheduler visit' in a test is precisely how a placement grid and a playback grid drift apart without anything going red — which is the bug above, stated as a process."

That bug silenced the 2nd and 3rd note of every triplet group, in every bar, in both clock modes, from the module's first commit — four of twelve positions sounded, and nothing looked wrong. The panel is the THIRD renderer of that grid. Zero re-typed constants; the layout constants move to score-layout.ts so the card and the panel cannot disagree about where a note is drawn.
